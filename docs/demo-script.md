# 90-second demo

“Flash Fam turns a Telegram signal into a user-signed trade, not a custodial black box.”

- `/start`: launch the companion.
- `/track 0x...`: follow a public wallet.
- `/copy 0x... 10`: choose alert mode and fixed size.
- Show the generated review card: route, amount, cap, and confirmation.
- `/mydca 0x... 10 1800`: Flash TWAP/DCA.
- `/mytp 0x... 2`: take-profit order.
- `/mystop 0x... 0.5`: stop-loss order.
- `/myorders`: reconcile resting orders.

The differentiator: social signal, advanced Flash order, and non-custodial signing in one Telegram flow.
